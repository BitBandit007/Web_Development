My Travel Blog

My second home is Bali, Indonesia, where I met my wife Intan, who’s a Bali local. Now she joins me on these adventures too. Together, we've done some bucket list hikes, climbed active volcanoes, seen exotic wildlife, and visited some spectacular castles, temples, and monuments around the world.
